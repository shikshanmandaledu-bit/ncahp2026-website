01 / About

A gathering point for the people who make diagnosis, care & recovery possible.

NCAHP 2026 brings together researchers, faculty, students, and practising healthcare professionals working across Medical Laboratory Sciences, Radiology, Operation Theatre Technology, Optometry, and Physiotherapy — the disciplines that sit closest to the patient, and furthest from the spotlight.

Hosted at AIIMS New Delhi, the conference is built as a working day for the allied health community: peer-reviewed research, hands-on demonstrations, and direct conversation between the clinicians running today's diagnostics and the innovators building tomorrow's.

07 / Abstract

Abstract Submission Guidelines for Paper Presentation

Abstracts are invited from Graduate, Postgraduate, and PhD students, as well as academicians, clinicians, and professionals.

Submission Details

  • Abstract submission will commence w.e.f. 15th August 2026
  • The abstract must be 250–300 words in length
  • Typed in Times New Roman, font size 12
  • Submitted in Microsoft Word format (.doc or .docx)
  • Submissions accepted through email only
  • All abstracts undergo peer review by the Scientific Committee

Presentation Guidelines

  • Only one author permitted to present the paper
  • Each presenter allotted 5–7 minutes for presentation
  • Plan for 2–3 minutes of questions
  • Final presentation prepared in PowerPoint (PPT) format
  • Slides submitted on or before 10 October 2026 via email

Guidelines for Poster Presentation

  • Maximum size: 3 feet (width) × 4 feet (height), portrait orientation
  • Content should be concise, visually appealing, and easy to read
  • Only one student allowed to present the poster
  • Only the title of the topic is required for poster presentation
  • Send at: ncahpaiims@gmail.com

Prizes

Prizes will be awarded for the best Innovation Poster and Scientific Paper Presentation.

Final Note: Conference registration is mandatory for all presentations.

Submit your abstract by email ncahpaiims@gmail.com
